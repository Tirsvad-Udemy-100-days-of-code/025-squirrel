# Squirrel Census

Day 25 of Udemy's *100 Days of Code: The Complete Python Pro Bootcamp*: count the squirrels of the 2018 Central Park Squirrel Census by primary fur colour (gray, cinnamon, black) and export the counts to `squirrel_count.csv`.
